# Compact fashion experience

## Latest layout

- Four destinations live in a floating rounded capsule: Home, Try On, Bag and You. Orders is a real segmented view inside Bag, with per-store and all-store history retained. The selected destination uses accent-coloured icon and text with a subtle inset capsule inside the thin glass dock. A separate colourful local-helper orb sits beside the capsule, not inside the tab list.
- Home search starts as a 32px icon in a 48px touch target, alongside category shortcuts and the matching compact Filters & sort control. This controls area and followed-brand rail stay fixed above the scrolling home feed. Opening it replaces that row with an editable field; closing retains the query in a visible chip. Filters opens the real catalogue options, not a decorative action. The duplicate Try On shortcut beside search and the “Denim, your way” promotional carousel are removed. Product, storefront and navigation Try On remain available.
- Electric ocean blue, citrus lemon, coral, sky and mint collection colours and small category badges create a fresher fashion feed without invented prices, discounts or live-stock claims. The incremental product grid arrives before promotional value sections. Small followed-brand marks and directory rows stay compact. Shared purple and muted terracotta styling are replaced by the brighter ocean/citrus palette without changing brand storefront identities. Normal-text contrast is at least 5.42:1 in light mode and 6.15:1 in dark mode; primary button labels exceed 6:1 in both.
- The dock now paints a 42px capsule instead of 60px, inside a 48px touch-safe row. Try On has a 28px disc, and the 40px painted helper orb retains a 48px target. The dock floats above real scrolling content, with no solid footer slab. Empty gaps are touch-through and end-of-scroll clearance keeps final actions reachable. Native uses translucency; the web preview also adds backdrop blur. Shared controls use the energetic blue/citrus Fashion Centre palette; individual storefront themes are preserved. Reduced-transparency preferences use a solid fallback. The dock respects safe insets, hides for keyboard input and scales with text. Four controls retain 48px targets; on extremely narrow windows the separate helper moves above the dock. The combined dock is bounded to 720px on large windows.
- Search is icon-first; expanded fields and their controls retain usable 44–48px editing/tap areas. Product Try On is a small 28px pill inside a 48px target. Normal catalogue text uses two columns at 320px, three at intermediate widths and four on tablets; large accessibility text falls back to one column.

## Reference-inspired visual polish

The supplied commerce references inform a soft ocean-blue header wash, underlined category selection and cleaner image-led product tiles. Product photographs have more room while captions are compact and borderless. The small Try On pill now sits on the photograph, as a separate accessible 48px action rather than a nested button, saving vertical space. All prices, availability and categories still come from actual loaded records. No ratings, discount percentages, bank offers, official logos or promotional claims were invented. The four-destination thin glass navigation, local helper, circular followed-brand links and fixed compact search/filter controls are retained. The removed promotional carousel is not reintroduced.

Saved-photo Try On now shows the selected garment and a calm next-step card instead of repeated headings and a large red sign-in warning. Missing sign-in and account photos are normal prerequisites; genuine verification failures use a small inline error with read-only retry. Private photo and generation consent controls stay hidden until account/photo verification succeeds. Brand-scoped palettes, service disclosure, explicit image-sharing confirmation and duplicate-submission protections remain. Backgrounding immediately clears private display state; recovery rechecks account/photo without resubmitting generation, and stale confirmations cannot act after re-verification.

## Incremental home feed

Home now owns one virtualized vertical list, separate from the pinned compact search/category/filter controls. The feed shows six unique products initially and appends six when approaching the end, with a manual Show more pieces fallback, a brief local append status, loading, retry, empty and explicit end states. Product and Try On actions keep exact product/store identities. Grid rows adapt from one to four columns with text size and measured pane width; scroll content retains dock and safe-area clearance. Duplicate records are keyed by store plus product ID, input order is preserved, and no items are repeated to manufacture an endless feed. Pending appends cancel on blur, unmount or changed catalogue identity.

This is progressive presentation of already-loaded data, not server pagination. The current catalogue endpoint supplies up to 48 products per store, a `more` flag and no continuation cursor; the isolated preview has ten fictional products. The UI clearly says when the loaded page ends and more products may exist. Retry/pull-to-refresh uses the existing catalogue reader, not a new provider or paid action. Discovery/value/story modules appear after the product feed ends so they do not interrupt automatic loading. Truly unbounded browsing requires a backend pagination contract and additional catalogue data.

## Compact circular brand links

The followed strip now uses 34px circular decorative rings around 26px original monograms, with 11px brand names below. Normal links are 64px wide and the row is 64px high; Discover is 56px wide and stays adjacent. Three followed brands plus Discover fit a 320px phone’s content area. Names and targets can grow for large text. Rings are decoration, not an unread-story or new-drop claim, and monograms are not official merchant logos. Only currently loaded followed brands appear. Existing motion safeguards, manual scrolling, discovery, empty/unavailable states and exact storefront navigation remain.

## Compact catalogue browsing

Explore-all browsing uses a small back/title/count row and one shared row for the search icon, horizontally scrolling categories and filter action. Search expands only when requested. Store, sort, currency and category behavior, active-option indicators, clear/reset, and product Try On remain available. Compact controls retain usable touch areas, readable text and focus handling. Browser QA measured the default home row at 54px and collapsed catalogue controls at 98px on a 320px window, down from the prior 140px catalogue area. Search open/type/Escape, retained query, filter handoff, sort/currency selection and full reset were exercised.

Bag, demo orders and checkout use tighter headers, line items and cards. Try On preparation, saved-photo screens and the You profile share smaller headings, spacing and cards without hiding account, photo, consent, privacy or generation checks. Image previews remain usable and aspect ratios are preserved.

## Local style helper

The smaller ocean-blue orb opens a compact, dismissible chat sheet that leaves more of the app visible. The transcript scrolls independently while input and close remain accessible. It searches only the currently loaded catalogue and respects store scope. Name/category queries and explicit-currency budgets return actual matching records with product links. Try On and Bag/Orders help open the existing flows. It cannot change the bag, place orders, upload photos or generate results.

This is explicitly labelled a local, rule-based demo, not live AI. Chat text is kept only in memory and clears on close or store/garment change. No messages or photos are sent to an AI service. Prices do not cross currencies. Unknown intent, empty/loading/error catalogues, stale products and ambiguous currency amounts have honest recovery messages.

## Personal profile and settings

You opens a compact personal profile, not the former settings menu. The top gear opens all retained account, appearance, device-data and merchant settings. Settings now uses compact grouped flat rows, monochrome outline icons, sentence-case labels and flexible 52px-minimum targets. Account and the clearly unconnected brand-owner dashboard remain near the top, while appearance, follows, shopping preferences and backup retain their actions. Actual identity comes only from a fresh authenticated account read, never from demo OTP data.

The existing backend has no account history-list API. The gallery therefore shows only successful jobs recorded during the current app session for the verified account. It does not invent completed results or claim historical sync. The in-memory list is bounded, clears on logout/account changes or app close, and stores no image bytes, signed URLs or credentials. Only jobs actually started and completed through this app can be indexed. Private image views unmount when leaving the profile, and account verification repeats on return or foregrounding. Failed verification hides results. Missing images offer retry.

The legacy result endpoint still needs server-side ownership/privacy hardening before production. Client account scoping is not a substitute for backend authorization. A signed-out or isolated browser preview shows the truthful empty/account-check state rather than seeded history.

## Retained behavior

- Only currently loaded followed stores appear. Monograms are local initials because the API has no official logo field. Brand follow remains separate from product saving.
- The followed strip moves only with overflow and at least two stores. Pause, manual scrolling and directional controls remain. Drag, focus, reduced motion, screen readers, inactive app and unfocused screens stop automatic movement.
- Each store keeps its own theme, scoped catalogue, search, bag and checkout, with back, switch-store and explicit exit controls.
- Wishlist UI, routes and save actions remain removed. Existing saved-product records and backups are untouched. Generic recommendations do not use those records.
- Local Try On preparation offers camera capture; its Choose from photos and replacement-picker options are removed. Existing account photo management, saved photos and user data are unchanged.
- Try On retains account, selected-garment, saved-photo, consent, service-readiness and generation checks. Opening Try On does not upload or generate anything.
- Native catalogue reads use the configured backend. The isolated web preview has ten fictional products, rejects all API writes and cannot sign in, upload a photo, purchase or run generation. Browser-local records remain separate from native storage.
